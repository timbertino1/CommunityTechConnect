# Security Policy

Security is the top priority for NETechConnect. Thank you for helping keep the project and its
visitors safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue for security problems.

Report privately through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose
**Report a vulnerability**. Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact you
expect.

We aim to acknowledge reports within **7 days** and will keep you informed while we work on a
fix. We are happy to credit reporters once a fix is released, if you would like.

## Scope

- The public website and its security headers
- The Cloudflare Workers (source suggestions and the newsletter inbox)
- The discovery pipeline and its GitHub Actions workflows (`discovery/`, `.github/`)

## Out of scope

- Content on third-party event pages that NETechConnect links to
- Denial-of-service or volumetric testing against the site or its hosting providers
- Findings that require a compromised GitHub or Cloudflare account
