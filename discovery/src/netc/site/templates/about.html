About NETechConnect

One place to find community tech events across Nebraska, starting with Omaha and Lincoln.

How events are found

Twice a day, a small program reads the public event calendars and event pages of Nebraska tech groups, universities and organizations. It keeps events that are about technology and happen in Nebraska (or online, hosted by a Nebraska group), merges the same event listed in two places, and links each one to the organizer's own page. New sources are reviewed by a person before their events appear.

NETechConnect does not sell tickets, take registrations or keep attendee lists. To go to an event, follow its link to the organizer.

Privacy

This site uses no cookies, no accounts and no tracking, and it loads nothing from other websites{% if suggest %}, except Cloudflare Turnstile's spam check on the suggestion form{% endif %}. We store only public event details: titles, times, places, organizers and short descriptions.
