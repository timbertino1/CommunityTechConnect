Follow new events

Get new events as they're listed, in an app you already use.

A feed is a web address that apps can check for new items. Copy one of the addresses below into a feed reader or another app that follows feeds, and it shows you each new event with a link to the organizer's page. There's nothing to sign up for here: NETechConnect doesn't collect email addresses or know who follows a feed.

Each feed lists upcoming events, newest first. A cancelled event stays in its feed with "Cancelled" in the title until its date passes.

Ways to use a feed

  • A feed reader (an app or website for following feeds): add the address as a new feed or subscription.
  • Slack: in a channel, type /feed subscribe followed by the address.
  • Classic Outlook for Windows: right-click RSS Subscriptions, choose Add a New RSS Feed, and paste the address.
  • Email: services that turn feeds into emails can send you new events. You sign up with the service you choose, not with NETechConnect.
